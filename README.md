##### **# Superstore Profit Leakage Analysis**

##### **### A Business Analytics Case Study | Tools: MySQL, Advanced Excel**



**## Project Overview**

###### A US-based retail company (Superstore) was experiencing declining profit margins

###### despite growing sales. As a Business Analyst, I was tasked with identifying the

###### root causes of profit leakage across regions, product categories, and customer

###### segments — and delivering actionable recommendations to management.

###### 

***## Tools Used***

***- MySQL Workbench — Data extraction and analysis***

***- Advanced Excel — Pivot Tables and data visualization***

***- GitHub — Project documentation and version control***





**## Business Problem**

Despite achieving over $2.2 million in total sales, the company's overall

profit margin remains inconsistent across regions and product categories.

Management needs to identify which areas are causing profit leakage and

what corrective actions can be taken.



\## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |


| Sales Director | Identify underperforming regions |

| Finance Manager | Understand profit margin gaps |

| Marketing Team | Optimize customer segment strategy |

| Operations Manager | Reduce loss-making product lines |



\## KPIs Tracked

\- Total Sales Revenue

\- Total Profit

\- Profit Margin %

\- Average Profit per Order

\- Sales and Profit by Region, Category, Segment



***## User Stories***



1\. **Sales Director** - I want to see total sales and profit margin

by region, so that I can identify which regions are underperforming

and reallocate resources accordingly.



2\. **Finance Manager** - I want to understand which product categories

have the lowest profit margins, so that I can flag pricing and cost issues.



3\. **Operations Manager** - I want to identify which products are

consistently sold at a loss, so that I can recommend discontinuation

or repricing.



4\. **Marketing Manager** - I want to understand the impact of discounts

on profitability, so that I can set guidelines for the sales team.



5\. **Marketing Manager** - I want to know which customer segment

generates the highest profit margin, so that I can focus acquisition

efforts on the most valuable segment.



**\*\*Finding 1 — Regional Performance:\*\***

Central region has the lowest profit margin of 8.06% despite generating

$497K in sales. West region leads with 14.85% margin. This gap suggests

heavy discounting or a product mix issue in Central that requires

immediate investigation.



**\*\*Finding 2 — Category Profitability:\*\***

Furniture is a high-volume, low-margin category with only 2.32% profit

margin despite $733K in sales. Technology leads with 17.39% margin.

The company is investing significant effort in Furniture but retaining

minimal profit.



**\*\*Finding 3 — Discount Impact:\*\***

Orders with no discount generate average profit of $68 per order.

Once discounts exceed 20%, profitability turns negative — medium

discounts cause losses of $78 per order and high discounts cause

losses of $111 per order.



**\*\*Finding 4 — Loss Making Products:\*\***

Five products show consistent losses. Cubify CubeX 3D Printer is the

worst performer with cumulative losses of $8,879. These products require

immediate repricing or discontinuation.



**\*\*Finding 5 — Customer Segment:\*\***

Home Office segment generates the highest profit margin of 14.05%

despite lowest sales volume. Consumer segment has highest sales but

lowest margin of 11.53% — proving that sales volume alone does not

indicate profitability.



**\*\*1. Fix the Discounting Strategy — Immediate Priority\*\***

Cap all discounts at 20% maximum across all product lines.

Orders with discounts above 20% are losing an average of $78-$111

per order. This single change could significantly improve overall

profitability.



**\*\*2. Investigate Central Region Operations\*\***

Central region's 8.06% profit margin is nearly half of West's 14.85%.

A detailed audit of Central region's discount practices and product

mix is recommended. Push higher-margin Technology products over

low-margin Furniture in this region.



**\*\*3. Review Furniture Pricing Strategy\*\***

Furniture generates $733K in sales but only 2.32% profit margin.

Either increase Furniture prices, reduce associated costs, or

reduce discounts on Furniture specifically. Consider shifting

marketing focus toward Technology which delivers 17.39% margin.



**\*\*4. Discontinue or Reprice Loss-Making Products\*\***

Five products are consistently sold at a loss. The Cubify CubeX

3D Printer alone has accumulated losses of $8,879. These products

should be repriced above cost or removed from the catalog entirely.



**\*\*5. Focus Customer Acquisition on Home Office Segment\*\***

Home Office customers deliver 14.05% profit margin — the highest

among all segments. Marketing efforts should prioritize acquiring

more Home Office customers over mass Consumer targeting.



**## Conclusion**

This analysis identified four key drivers of profit leakage —

excessive discounting, low-margin Furniture category, underperforming

Central region, and loss-making products. Implementing the above

recommendations could significantly improve the company's overall

profit margin from the current average of 11-14% range.
